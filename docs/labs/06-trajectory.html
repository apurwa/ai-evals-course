Trajectory scorer

Define what a correct path looks like, score 120 real traces against it, then see the gap between what the agent can do and what it does reliably.

Part 1: define the path

required and forbidden

Tick the tools that must appear for each kind of request. Your definition is scored against all 120 traces immediately. Note that you are not writing a golden transcript: order is not checked, only presence, because several orders are acceptable.

Ground truth here is the corpus label. A trajectory rule that disagrees with it is not automatically wrong: you may have encoded a stricter policy than the corpus does. The disagreements are the interesting part, which is why they are listed below.

Part 2: capable versus reliable

real per-kind rates

Per-kind success rates below are measured from the corpus. The two curves are what those rates imply if a customer needs the agent to succeed k times in a row, against what a research benchmark would report for k attempts.

pass@k, capability, at least one of k works pass^k, reliability, all k work
