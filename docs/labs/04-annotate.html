Lab 4/browser/60 min or more

Annotation tool

Read traces, name what went wrong in your own words, and watch your saturation curve decide when you are done. Labels stay hidden until you ask for them.

Resist the urge to reveal early

There is a button at the bottom that shows the corpus's own failure labels. Pressing it before you have coded thirty or forty traces will replace your categories with its categories, and you will learn nothing except how to agree. Your work is saved locally, so leaving and coming back is fine.
