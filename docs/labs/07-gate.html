Lab 7/browser/~25 min

Gate designer

Set thresholds, run eight candidate releases through them, and count how often you block something that was fine.

How the releases are made

Each release is a deterministic resample of the real 120-trace corpus, mixed to hit a target success rate. Five are healthy, at 89 to 93 percent. Three carry a genuine regression and fail disproportionately in one specific way, which is what a real regression looks like. Because the sample is random, a healthy release can still look bad and a regressed one can still slip through: that is not a flaw in the lab, it is the entire problem with gating on a small sample, and it is what you are here to feel.
