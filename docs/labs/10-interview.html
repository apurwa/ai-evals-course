Lab 10/browser/~45 min

Interview drill

Twelve questions about work you have actually done, each with a rubric for what a strong answer contains. Saved in your browser as you type.

How to use this

Write your answer before opening the rubric. Reading it first turns this into a comprehension exercise, which is the same mistake the L4 lab warns about. Answers save automatically and stay on this machine.

Portfolio summary

assembled from your answers

Generates a plain-text summary from what you wrote, ready to paste into a document, a README, or a message to a hiring manager. Nothing leaves your browser.
